# Split Room — Design direction

## Product surface

The product is a local A/B experiment bench: a visitor adjusts traffic allocation, simulates exposure, records conversions, and reads the result without a false claim of production analytics.

## Visual world

An experiment control room built from a paper worksheet and a small phosphor signal. The page is a weighting room rather than a SaaS dashboard: one split table, one honest readout, and enough margin for the numbers to be questioned.

## Direction seed

Impeccable direction seed `42bcd5c8`, operate mode, assigned direction 5. The grounded interpretation is a traffic-split instrument with acid allocation bars, orange annotations, and a quiet paper surface.

## System

- Palette: warm paper `#f0eee8`, ink `#202629`, acid `#b8d338`, orange `#d66d3b`, panel `#e2e4de`.
- Typography: Manrope for the interface and JetBrains Mono for allocation, status, and evidence labels.
- Composition: oversized question-led hero, single split table, narrow readout rail; no generic card grid.
- Interaction: weighted random simulation, direct allocation sliders, conversion controls, seed reset, local persistence.
- Responsive: the readout drops below the experiment and each variant action remains reachable without horizontal scrolling.

## Honesty constraints

This is a local instrument. It must never imply real visitors, statistical significance, cookies, or external analytics.
